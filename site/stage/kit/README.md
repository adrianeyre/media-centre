# The Stageshow kit

The React parts the website's illustrations are drawn with: the stage, its
script and cursor, windows and phones, and the motion and small parts. It is
the `kit/` folder of [Stageshow](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/tree/main/stageshow),
by Benji Taylor, under the MIT licence in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), copied as it is
from commit `802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572` (31 October 2026), with
its [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md). Its brand artwork's sources and licences are in
[`brands/SOURCES.md`](brands/SOURCES.md).

It is not edited here, so that it can be replaced by a newer copy; the scenes in
`../scenes/` style its parts with the Remote's look (`../look.css`). Prettier
and ESLint leave it alone.
